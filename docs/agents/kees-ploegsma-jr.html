🇳🇱 Kees Ploegsma Jr.

Dutch agent named in Keisuke Honda's 2009 sale from VVV-Venlo to CSKA Moscow, and quoted as Honda's agent through 2010. An example of a local agent taking over for the big sale.

📌 1. In short

When Honda was ready to leave Venlo for a big club, the agent named in the deal was a Dutchman with local contacts, not the Japanese agent from his arrival. Nothing found says whether Ploegsma replaced Tetsuro Kiyooka or worked with him.

⚠️ 3. Controversies and criticism

Two items came up. Neither could be fully confirmed.
  • A 2009 Dutch report says he was among agents accused, in a case before the Dutch FA, of approaching players younger than 16. I did not find the outcome. [source]
  • An agent-directory website says his work with Honda at CSKA Moscow led to a sports-court case over unpaid commission and oral agreements. I found no court document or news report to confirm it. [source]

💡 4. What we can learn (my analysis)

  • The agent who gets you in is not always the agent who gets you up. A big sale needs someone the buying clubs already know.
  • Honda changed representation at each stage: a Japanese agent to arrive, a Dutch agent for the sale, his own brother later. Yoshida's advice to choose an agent carefully fits this pattern.
