🧢 VC Sports Group VCスポーツグループ

An American baseball agency best known for Dodgers star Mookie Betts. It has become a second route to MLB for Japanese players, next to the bigger Wasserman (THE·TEAM): it handled pitcher Tomoyuki Sugano's moves and, in 2026, the largest contract ever reported for a Japanese position player.

📌 Status (2026-11-27): Active. On 27 Nov 2026 its client Teruaki Sato's 6-year contract with the Chicago Cubs was announced, reported at $120 million. This page is a short first version: the public record I could open is thin.

📌 1. In short

Most top Japanese pitchers in MLB use one agency, Wasserman. Sato chose a different one about two years before he left Japan. That agency talked to his Japanese club for him, which is still unusual in Japan, and finished his MLB deal in 23 days.

🎯 4. Method: what it did for Sato

  • It negotiated with the Japanese club, not only with MLB clubs. Sato said his agent and Hanshin talked through posting and salary together. [source]
  • It used the yearly contract as the moment to press. Sato stayed unsigned until 30 Jan 2026, then signed for one year only, with posting talks continuing. [source]
  • It moved fast once the window opened: posted 1 Nov, agreed 24 Nov, ahead of the 1 Dec labour deadline. [source]
  • It connected him with a star client: he trained with Mookie Betts in Dec 2025 (headline only). [source]
  • The result beat the market forecast: MLB Trade Rumors had predicted 5 years and $80 million. [source]

⚠️ 6. Controversies and criticism

None found about the agency itself (searched 27 Nov 2026; a light check).
  • Tabloid framing: Nikkan Gendai wrote of Sato 'forcing through' a move, and other tabloids called his training with Betts a Dodgers attempt to fence him in. Neither happened: Hanshin approved the posting and he signed with the Cubs. [source]
  • The other side of a hard negotiation: Sankei Sports described player and club as running in parallel lines in January 2026. The club's answer was that a player's wish alone is never enough. [source]

🔭 7. Lessons (my analysis)

  • A hitter does not have to use the agency the pitchers use. A record contract for a star hitter (Betts) was the selling point here.
  • Hiring the agent two years early mattered more than the 23 days of bidding. The hard part was getting the Japanese club to say yes.
  • If Morishita really has signed too, Hanshin now face the same agency for their next star. Worth watching.
