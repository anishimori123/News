🚪 Hai Berden ハイ・ベルデン

Dutch logistics businessman who, as chairman of small club VVV-Venlo, gave Keisuke Honda, Maya Yoshida and others their first step into European football. A 'gateway' person: he is the reason the Dutch door existed.

📌 Status (2026-11-21): Not an agent. Former chairman of Dutch club VVV-Venlo (2003–2018) and founder of Seacon Logistics. Returned as a co-owner of the club in early 2020.

📌 1. In short

Berden is the answer to 'why Venlo?' He ran a shipping and warehouse company that did business with Japan, he also ran the local football club, and he joined the two. He asked a Dutch coach working in Japan for a talented player, got Honda, sold him for millions, and repeated the idea with Yoshida.

🇯🇵 3. What he said about the Japanese players

  • Honda: a very strong personality from the first meeting, which Berden credits for his success. He compared Honda's ambition and rebellious streak to Dutch legend Johan Cruyff. [source]
  • He said he had never seen such a character at VVV. [source]
  • Yoshida: more of a 'typical Japanese' type than Honda. Serious, disciplined, responsible, and cheerful. Berden took him to a local pop concert when he arrived. [source]
  • Otsu: Berden had followed him since his time at a German club and watched him at the London Olympics before signing him. [source]
  • Toshiya Fujita wrote that Berden's support let him stay at the club for three and a half years and become a coach, and that Berden came to his farewell match in Tokyo. [source]

💶 4. Why it worked as a business

  • Buy unknown, sell proven. Honda arrived as a 21-year-old few in Europe knew and left two years later for €6 million to €9 million, depending on the source. [source]
  • Yoshida was sold to Southampton for about £3 million. [source]
  • The same man was chairman, main sponsor and stadium sponsor, so decisions were fast. [source]
  • Trust came from a coach who knew both worlds: a Dutchman from the same region, working in Japan. [source]
  • Care off the pitch: the club's staff greet Japanese visitors in Japanese, and a Japanese staff member worked there by 2014. [source]

💡 6. What we can learn (my analysis)

  • A gateway can be one person's hobby and business meeting. No federation plan or agent strategy created the Dutch route.
  • Small clubs are good first steps because the player plays every week. Honda even dropped to the second division and used it to grow.
  • The model later became an industry: Sint-Truiden in Belgium was bought by a Japanese company in 2017 to do on purpose what Venlo did by accident.
