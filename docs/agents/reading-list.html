📌 1. In short

Robert Whiting is an American who has lived in Tokyo for most of the last sixty years and is widely called the leading English-language writer on Japanese baseball. His books are the background to three pages on this site: Hideo Nomo, Don Nomura, and (through a reported friendship) Shawn Novak.

🧑‍💼 4. Who is Robert Whiting?

  • Born in New Jersey in 1942, raised in Eureka, California. Came to Tokyo with the US Air Force in 1962 and studied at Sophia University. [source]
  • Best known for You Gotta Have Wa, which Wikipedia says sold 125,000 copies; the writer David Halberstam said it applies to almost every side of US–Japan relations. [source]
  • Winner of SABR's Henry Chadwick Award for baseball research. [source]
  • Still active: he gave a lecture on baseball and US–Japan diplomacy at Occidental College in Los Angeles on 9 Sept 2025. [source]
  • Link to this site (RUMOUR, not fact): Weekly Jitsuwa quoted an unnamed insider in Feb 2026 saying agent Shawn Novak is close to him. [source]

⚠️ 5. Controversies and criticism

None found about Whiting himself (27 Nov 2026; not searched in depth).
  • A caution this site already applies: on the Nomo page some details rest on Whiting alone. He is the standard source, but one author is still one author. My observation. [source]
  • His books side with the players against the clubs, and the 1999 book is built on Don Nomura's own account. Read them as one side of the argument, the side this site's club sources do not give. My observation.
