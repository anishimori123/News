# 🚪 Hai Berden (ハイ・ベルデン)

## 📌 1. In short
Berden is the answer to 'why Venlo?' He ran a shipping and warehouse company that did business with Japan, he also ran the local football club, and he joined the two. He asked a Dutch coach working in Japan for a talented player, got Honda, sold him for millions, and repeated the idea with Yoshida.

## 🇯🇵 3. What he said about the Japanese players
- Honda: a very strong personality from the first meeting, which Berden credits for his success. He compared Honda's ambition and rebellious streak to Dutch legend Johan Cruyff. ([source](https://number.bunshun.jp/articles/-/813731))
- He said he had never seen such a character at VVV. ([source](https://www.vi.nl/nieuws/hoe-vvv-kennismaakte-met-een-onvergetelijk-japans-fenomeen))
- Yoshida: more of a 'typical Japanese' type than Honda. Serious, disciplined, responsible, and cheerful. Berden took him to a local pop concert when he arrived. ([source](https://number.bunshun.jp/articles/-/813744))
- Otsu: Berden had followed him since his time at a German club and watched him at the London Olympics before signing him. ([source](https://sportiva.shueisha.co.jp/clm/football/wfootball/2012/10/24/post_184/))
- Toshiya Fujita wrote that Berden's support let him stay at the club for three and a half years and become a coach, and that Berden came to his farewell match in Tokyo. ([source](https://number.bunshun.jp/articles/-/830795))

## 💶 4. Why it worked as a business
- Buy unknown, sell proven. Honda arrived as a 21-year-old few in Europe knew and left two years later for €6 million to €9 million, depending on the source. ([source](https://www.voetbalzone.nl/nieuws/honda-vertrekt-voor-vier-jaar-naar-cska-moskou/93778))
- Yoshida was sold to Southampton for about £3 million. ([source](https://www.goal.com/jp/%E3%83%8B%E3%83%A5%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9/maya-yoshida-saints/blt22801bb776d61a11))
- The same man was chairman, main sponsor and stadium sponsor, so decisions were fast. ([source](https://venlo.nieuws.nl/nieuws/21-inwoners-gemeente-venlo-gedecoreerd-tijdens-lintjesregen))
- Trust came from a coach who knew both worlds: a Dutchman from the same region, working in Japan. ([source](https://www.vi.nl/nieuws/hoe-vvv-kennismaakte-met-een-onvergetelijk-japans-fenomeen))
- Care off the pitch: the club's staff greet Japanese visitors in Japanese, and a Japanese staff member worked there by 2014. ([source](https://number.bunshun.jp/articles/-/813744))

## 💡 6. What we can learn (my analysis)
- A gateway can be one person's hobby and business meeting. No federation plan or agent strategy created the Dutch route.
- Small clubs are good first steps because the player plays every week. Honda even dropped to the second division and used it to grow.
- The model later became an industry: Sint-Truiden in Belgium was bought by a Japanese company in 2017 to do on purpose what Venlo did by accident.
